# Voice Studio — Probably

**Over-engineered anti-AI-slop generation. Probably.**

A self-narrated working-build demo. Voice Studio generates the very dialogue you hear while the recording shows the app creating it.

[Watch the demo](./Voice-Studio-Probably.mp4)

The voices are **Nova Prime** and **Atlas Deep**, both synthetic sample voices. The soundtrack was generated in the app as nine dialogue blocks, arranged on separate speaker tracks, and exported as a WAV. The screen recording shows dialogue generation, transcription back into editable text, non-destructive cleanup, a description-based voice sample, arrangement/export, and the visual player.

Generation waits are fast-forwarded. No explanatory captions or subtitles were added. The final video uses the app-generated mix as its voiceover.

This is a development-build preview, not a release or version-number declaration. It includes local transcription and voice-design integration that may be ahead of the published source. No personal recordings are used in the demo.

## Files

- `Voice-Studio-Probably.mp4` — the finished narrated video.
- `Voice-Studio-voiceover.wav` — the exact voiceover mix exported by the app.
- `dialogue.json` — speaker assignments and narration text.
- `poster.jpg` — a frame from the demo.

The dialogue includes profanity. All media stays in this repository; no third-party video host is required.
